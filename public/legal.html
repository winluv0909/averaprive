Avera Privé / LEGAL

Clear rules.
Responsible operation.

This launch draft explains the intended platform rules. It must be reviewed and completed with the registered business identity, jurisdiction, contact details, retention periods, and final service providers before public production launch.

PRIVACY

Use data only for a defined purpose.

Avera Privé is designed to collect account, agency profile, inquiry, review-evidence, verification, support, security, and payment-administration information only when needed to provide and protect the service. Private evidence must remain restricted to authorized reviewers. Production policy must specify the controller, lawful bases, recipients, international transfers, retention schedule, user rights, deletion process, and privacy contact.

TERMS

Listings are information, not guarantees.

Users remain responsible for evaluating agencies, creators, contracts, legal compliance, and commercial decisions. Verification confirms only the checks described in the published methodology. Subscriptions and sponsored placement do not buy verification, favorable reviews, or objective rank. The final terms must identify the contracting entity, governing law, payment and cancellation rules, liability terms, dispute process, and age eligibility.

COOKIES

Essential first; optional only with choice.

Authentication and security storage may be required for the service to function. Analytics, advertising, and other optional technologies must not be activated until they are accurately disclosed and, where required, the visitor has provided consent. A production consent manager should expose clear accept, reject, and preference controls.

ACCEPTABLE USE

Protect people and the integrity of the marketplace.

Impersonation, fabricated evidence, manipulated reviews, harassment, unlawful content, credential abuse, automated scraping, deceptive sponsored content, and attempts to bypass platform security are prohibited. Avera Privé may restrict content or accounts while credible safety, fraud, or legal concerns are reviewed, with actions recorded in the appropriate audit trail.
