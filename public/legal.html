This launch draft explains the intended platform rules. It must be reviewed and completed with the actual operator identity, jurisdiction, contact details, retention periods, and final service providers before public production launch.

PRIVACY

Use data only for a defined purpose.

Avera Privé is designed to collect account, agency profile, inquiry, review-evidence, verification, support, security, and payment-administration information only when needed to provide and protect the service. Private evidence must remain restricted to authorized reviewers. Production policy must specify the controller, lawful bases, recipients, international transfers, retention schedule, user rights, deletion process, and privacy contact.

TERMS

Listings are information, not guarantees.

Users remain responsible for evaluating agencies, creators, contracts, legal compliance, and commercial decisions. Verification confirms only the checks described in the published methodology. Subscriptions and sponsored placement do not buy verification, favorable reviews, or objective rank. The final terms must identify the contracting operator, governing law, payment and cancellation rules, liability terms, dispute process, and age eligibility.

COOKIES

Essential first; optional only with choice.

Authentication and security storage may be required for the service to function. Analytics, advertising, and other optional technologies must not be activated until they are accurately disclosed and, where required, the visitor has provided consent. Optional directory analytics are disabled by default. Choose Essential only or Allow directory analytics in the privacy panel; use Cookie Settings to change your choice. Choosing essential only stops future optional directory event collection. Contact us about previously collected records.

ACCEPTABLE USE

Protect people and the integrity of the marketplace.

Impersonation, fabricated evidence, manipulated reviews, harassment, unlawful content, credential abuse, automated scraping, deceptive sponsored content, and attempts to bypass platform security are prohibited. Avera Privé may restrict content or accounts while credible safety, fraud, or legal concerns are reviewed, with actions recorded in the appropriate audit trail.

BILLING OPERATIONS

Payment and refund information.

Plan purchases are for a specified period and do not renew automatically. Sandbox transactions are test records. Paid placement and subscriptions do not purchase verification, positive reviews or objective rankings.

For current wallet invoices, the temporary process for verified underpayments, overpayments and late payments is refund and retry. Contact billing support with the invoice reference and transaction hash. An owner checks payer ownership and finalized deposit evidence before opening a case. The old unpaid invoice is cancelled and voided. Do not send another payment to it.

Refunds are arranged manually outside this website. The current process returns the full received amount in the same asset and network, with network fees paid separately by the operator. An outgoing refund record is not a guarantee that funds have arrived. Confirm receipt before creating a new invoice. Incorrect token or network transfers, paid-invoice refunds and other cases need individual support review; recovery cannot be promised.

This describes the current workflow, not a complete refund policy or a limit on rights that apply to you. The draft terms and privacy policy above still require the actual operator details and final review.
