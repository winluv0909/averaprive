Our public labels describe different things. A subscription does not grant verification, reviews, or a Top Rated ranking.

Launch status: The directory has no approved agencies. Trust badges and ratings will appear only after the corresponding review processes are operating.

Three distinct labels

01 / INDEPENDENT CHECK

Verified

Company identity, public presence, an authorized representative, and supporting business information are reviewed by a person. Approved applications can receive a badge; missing or failed checks cannot. A paid plan has no role in the decision.

02 / PAID VISIBILITY

Featured / Sponsored

An active paid placement appears with a visible disclosure wherever it is shown. It does not imply verification, a positive review, or an objective ranking. It sits outside organic rankings.

03 / REVIEW SIGNAL

Top Rated

Eligibility requires at least five published reviews from distinct, evidence checked clients, an average of at least 4.5 out of 5 across all published reviews, and no unresolved serious trust report. Paid plans have no influence.

VERIFICATION

How an agency is verified

  1. Apply. An authorized agency representative submits company identity, website, contact, and supporting business evidence through a secure account workflow.
  2. Check. A reviewer checks the documentation, website control, business presence, and the representative's authority. Missing or inconsistent information triggers follow up.
  3. Decide. A reviewer records approval, rejection, or a request for more information and keeps an audit trail. Verification may be revoked after material changes or substantiated reports.
  4. Display. The badge appears only while an approved verification decision remains valid. It never means Avera Privé guarantees performance.
REVIEWS

Publish feedback carefully

Submission

Reviewers must identify themselves privately and describe a genuine first hand business relationship. One active review per reviewer and agency; promotional content, impersonation, threats, and conflicts of interest are prohibited.

Moderation

New reviews stay pending. Moderators check relationship evidence, policy compliance, duplicate accounts, and suspicious patterns before publication. Rejected and hidden reviews are excluded from public averages.

Response

An agency can respond without changing the original review. Responses are moderated. A contested review can be temporarily hidden while evidence is examined, then restored or removed with a recorded decision.

REPORTS & DISPUTES

Fair handling when information is challenged

  1. A visitor or agency submits a report identifying the profile or review and what is inaccurate, abusive, fraudulent, or outdated.
  2. Staff acknowledge the report, preserve the original record and evidence privately, and restrict content during serious investigations.
  3. The other party may provide a response and supporting evidence. The reviewer handling the dispute must not be the subject of it.
  4. Staff record a reasoned resolution: restore, correct, hide, remove, revoke verification, or refer for further investigation. Both parties receive the outcome and can request reconsideration.

The reporting form and case notifications require secure backend services. Until connected, no website action should imply that a report was filed.

FRAUD PREVENTION

Checks behind the public labels

Account verification, submission limits, duplicate relationship detection, suspicious review bursts, shared device and network signals where lawful, and a moderation queue reduce abuse. Evidence remains private. Suspected review fraud pauses publication and Top Rated eligibility during investigation. Staff actions are logged.

Top Rated calculation

Use only published reviews from distinct reviewers. The displayed average is the arithmetic mean rounded to one decimal. Agencies need five evidence checked client reviews and an unrounded average of at least 4.5. Recalculate after every moderation decision; clear eligibility while a serious report is unresolved. Ties in Highest Rated sorting use published review count, then agency name. Review data and placement are never purchased.
