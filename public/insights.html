EDITORIAL STANDARDS

Trust the label. Check the evidence.

01 / ACCURACY

Verify before publishing

Names, claims, figures and quotations require appropriate sources. Material uncertainty is stated and significant corrections are dated.

02 / INDEPENDENCE

Commercial relationships are disclosed

A subscription, directory placement or advertising purchase does not guarantee favorable coverage or editorial inclusion.

03 / CLEAR LABELS

Editorial and paid work stay distinct

Paid material is labelled Sponsored or Partner Content. Editorial features remain subject to review even when included as a plan opportunity.

04 / FAIRNESS

Right of reply and corrections

Subjects of material criticism should receive a reasonable opportunity to respond. Readers and subjects will be able to report factual concerns.

05 / CARE

Sensitive coverage needs stronger safeguards

Adult industry reporting requires consent-aware language, privacy protection and care around exploitation, safety and legal claims.

06 / TRANSPARENCY

Methods belong beside market claims

Market analysis should state its sources, coverage period, sample limitations and how conclusions were reached.
