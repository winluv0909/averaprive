No. Paid plans may include clearly labelled promotional placement, but objective rankings use a separate documented methodology.

No. Agency discovery and comparison remain free for creators.

No. Avera Privé will measure and report marketplace activity honestly and will not promise lead volume before it is demonstrated.

These are recommended launch prices. Future pricing may evolve as traffic, lead quality, and measurable agency ROI develop.
