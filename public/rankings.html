02 / REVIEW BASED RANKINGS

Earned recognition.

Top Rated is intended for agencies with enough published, evidence checked client reviews. It is distinct from identity verification and paid visibility.

Status: Rankings have not launched. There are no eligible agencies or published ranked results.

Top Rated eligibility

  • Approved and publicly listed agency.
  • At least five published reviews from distinct, evidence checked clients.
  • Average rating of at least 4.5 out of 5 across all published reviews.
  • No unresolved serious trust report.

Meeting the criteria determines eligibility; it does not imply guaranteed results for clients.

OUR PUBLISHED RULES

Ranking methodology

These are the rules we intend to apply when review based rankings launch. We will show the first calculation date and last update date when results are published.

01

Which signals count?

Eligibility uses approval status, distinct evidence checked published reviews, average rating, and unresolved serious trust reports. Among eligible agencies, sort by the average of all published ratings, highest first. Break equal averages by number of published reviews, then agency name A to Z.

02

How often does it change?

Planned schedule: recalculate every Monday at 00:00 UTC after review moderation and serious report checks. An agency that loses eligibility should be removed when the decision is recorded. The live ranking page will display its latest calculation date once launched.

03

What does not count?

Subscription tier, advertising spend, sponsored placement, website clicks, inquiry volume, and sales claims do not affect review ranking or verification decisions. Results and case studies require separate substantiation and are not ranking inputs.

04

How are reviews protected?

Reviews require evidence of a client relationship and moderation before publication. Suspected duplicates, conflicts, manipulation, and fraud are investigated. Agencies may report or dispute a review; serious unresolved reports suspend Top Rated eligibility while reviewed. See trust principles.

Sponsored placement is separate

If sponsored or featured agencies are shown near the index, they must appear in a separate area labelled “Featured / Sponsored.” They do not receive an organic rank number, move within the organic list, or receive Verified or Top Rated automatically.
