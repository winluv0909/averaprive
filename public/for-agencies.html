COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you choose a plan.

Does a paid plan guarantee leads?

No. Paid tools and disclosed exposure may increase opportunities to be seen, but inquiries and outcomes depend on demand and fit.

Can we buy a Verified badge or a higher rank?

No. Verification is based on independent checks. Review based rankings follow the published methodology, and paid placements are separate.

Is a homepage or editorial feature guaranteed?

No. Homepage inventory is subject to availability. Editorial features require review; the commercial relationship must be disclosed. The exact placement and schedule need to be agreed before purchase.

When do billing and analytics begin?

Billing and analytics are not yet active in this site. The plan selection interface currently does not charge you. Availability will be stated before any subscription starts.
